# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project is built by people, for people. We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who participates — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We value technical excellence, but never at the expense of treating people well.

## Expected Behavior

Participants in this project are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in speech and actions, in code review comments as much as in discussions.
- Assume good intent. Most disagreements are misunderstandings, not malice.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect differing viewpoints, experience levels, and backgrounds.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize when appropriate, and learn from the experience.
- Prioritize what is best for the project and the community over personal preference.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat, events, or any other channel associated with this project):

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Insulting, demeaning, or derogatory comments; personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information (such as a physical or email address) without explicit permission ("doxxing").
- Trolling, deliberate disruption of discussions, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Plagiarism or misrepresenting others' work as your own.
- Any other conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces — for example, using an official project email address, posting from an official social media account, or acting as a designated representative at an event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers at **code@shaftware.com**.

All reports will be handled with discretion. We will respect the privacy and security of the reporter, and we will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

When reporting, it helps to include:

- Your contact information (so we can follow up).
- Names or usernames of the people involved.
- A description of what happened, with links to relevant issues, comments, or messages if available.
- Any additional context you believe is relevant.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Depending on the severity and frequency of the behavior, maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including:

1. **Correction** — A private, written reminder of the expected behavior, with an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning** — A formal warning, possibly with conditions such as avoiding interaction with affected parties for a period of time.
3. **Temporary Ban** — A temporary ban from participation in the project and its community spaces.
4. **Permanent Ban** — Permanent removal from all project spaces for repeated violations, harassment, or egregious behavior.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face the same consequences, as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

## Questions

If anything in this document is unclear, open a discussion or contact the maintainers at **code@shaftware.com**. We'd rather answer a question than have someone stay silent out of uncertainty.
